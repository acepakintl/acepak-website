Message from Chief Executive

Established in January 2023, AcePak International is an engineer-owned construction and engineering company committed to delivering reliable, high-quality, and sustainable infrastructure solutions across Northern Pakistan.

Our expertise spans RCC, stone, timber, and mud-brick construction, supported by practical site experience and modern construction equipment. We develop solutions suited to challenging terrain, harsh winters, and remote locations.

We believe in quality workmanship, professional integrity, sustainable practices, and strong client relationships. Our commitment is to deliver durable infrastructure that meets our clients’ needs and creates lasting value for the communities we serve.

Engr. Shafi UllahChief ExecutiveAcePak International (Private) Limited
